Open exercises from the lesson, to try on your own. They have no answer files: work them out, and use the labs above as reference.

### Your turn: rewrite one comment

1. Take one vague comment from a past review.
2. Name the risk, location and evidence it lacks.
3. Rewrite it as one question and one next action.

> **Hint:** If the author can reply only with what do you want, the comment needs more detail.

## Check yourself

- What three parts make a comment actionable?
- How does a question improve a review?
- Why separate preferences from blockers?
- What should the author be able to do after reading a comment?
