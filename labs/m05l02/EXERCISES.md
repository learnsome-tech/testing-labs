# Exercises — What To Look For In A Review

Lesson `m05l02` · [Watch](https://learnsome.tech/courses/testing-course/watch?lesson=m05l02)

## Exercise 1: Your turn: review one risky diff

1. Choose a recent change with an external boundary.
2. Write one question about its failure path and one about operations.
3. Ask for the smallest evidence that answers each question.

> **Hint**: A good review comment points to a risk, a location and a next action.
